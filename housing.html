Veterans Justice League Housing

VJL describes its housing program as a structured approach to empowering Veterans coming out of the criminal justice system so they can successfully transition back into society.

Program Structure

Physical trainingPeer groupsCheck-insHouse meetingsWeekly room inspectionsMonthly progress review

The current program description says residents take part in multiple physical training sessions throughout the week, peer groups, check-ins, house meetings, weekly room inspections, and monthly progress reviews.

VJL Housing

Veteran-specific transitional housing.

Community Service

VJL identifies monthly community volunteer work as an important part of the housing program. Published examples include a Disabled American Veterans post cleanup and the Team RWB Christmas Ruck to Children’s Hospital.

After the monthly volunteer event requirement is met, VJL describes taking the volunteer team to a monthly social activity, with examples such as a Nuggets game, concerts, and Top Golf.

Physical Training

VJL currently states that weekly community workouts are held Saturdays at 8:00 AM with Veteran residents and community members including justice-involved Veterans, first responders, Veterans, and active-duty service members. The site says this is typically located at The Phoenix gym near Old Town.
