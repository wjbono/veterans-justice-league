VJL Housing

Structure, support, and a place to move forward

Veteran-specific housing helps justice-involved Veterans build stability while transitioning from the criminal justice system back into the community.

VJL HousingStability · Accountability · Community

Who it supports

Built for the transition back

The program is designed around justice-involved Veterans coming out of the criminal justice system. It combines housing with routines, peer connection, personal accountability, and community involvement.

Is this the right starting point?

If you are seeking housing for yourself or helping a Veteran explore options, contact VJL directly. The team can explain current availability, program expectations, and next steps.

How the program works

Support with clear expectations

Published program information describes a structured weekly rhythm designed to help residents build consistency and make measurable progress.

01

Physical training

Multiple training sessions throughout the week help build routine, wellness, and shared accountability.

02

Peer support

Peer groups, check-ins, and house meetings create regular opportunities for connection and honest conversation.

03

House accountability

Weekly room inspections and shared expectations support a stable, respectful living environment.

04

Progress reviews

Monthly reviews give residents and the program a consistent point to assess progress and next steps.

Beyond the house

Connection through service and activity

Monthly community volunteer work is an important part of the program. Published examples include a Disabled American Veterans post cleanup and the Team RWB Christmas Ruck to Children’s Hospital.

VJL also describes monthly social activities after volunteer requirements are met, giving the team another way to build positive community connections.

Service

Give back together

Volunteer work connects residents with the broader community and a shared purpose.

Community

Build healthy connection

Group activities reinforce relationships beyond formal program requirements.

Community workouts

Training alongside the community

Published VJL information describes weekly Saturday morning workouts with Veteran residents and community members, including justice-involved Veterans, first responders, Veterans, and active-duty service members.

Workout times and locations may change. Contact VJL before planning to attend.
