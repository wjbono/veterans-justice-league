About Veterans Justice League

Mission & Vision

Support, housing, advocacy, intervention, prevention, and stabilization for justice-involved Veterans and active-duty service members.

Mission

To provide support, housing, and advocacy for justice involved Veterans and allow them to use their unique stories to impact change among active-duty service members.

Vision

VJL describes a three-fold vision:

1. Intervention

Create true intervention for justice-involved Veterans moving from incarceration back into society.

2. Prevention

Create true prevention within the military ranks by expanding work with military service members, with a vision of expanding the program to other bases.

3. Stabilization

Create true stabilization for justice-involved Veterans by helping them tap back into the personal potential they displayed while serving their country.
