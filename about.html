About Veterans Justice League

Veterans turning experience into impact

VJL supports justice-involved Veterans while using lived experience to strengthen re-entry, prevention, and connection with the military community.

Explore ProgramsMeet the Team

Support · Housing · Advocacy

Our mission

To provide support, housing, and advocacy for justice involved Veterans and allow them to use their unique stories to impact change among active-duty service members.

Our three-part vision

Support the transition. Prevent the next crisis.

01

Re-entry

Intervention

Create meaningful intervention for justice-involved Veterans moving from incarceration back into society.

02

Military community

Prevention

Use lived experience to support prevention among service members and expand the work to additional military communities.

03

Long-term progress

Stabilization

Help justice-involved Veterans reconnect with the potential, purpose, and strengths they demonstrated while serving.

Mission in action

One mission, three connected program areas

Housing builds stability. Behind-the-Wall Training turns lived experience into prevention. Outreach connects Veterans with resources and community before and after re-entry.
