VJL Outreach

Connect earlier. Re-enter stronger.

Prison visits, Veteran-resource education, and community partnerships help justice-involved Veterans prepare for a more stable return.

The goal

Create stability before release

VJL brings information and community connections to Veterans behind the wall, helping them understand resources that may be available because of their Veteran status before they return to society.

Working one Veteran at a time

Earlier information, trusted relationships, and stronger community connections can help make the re-entry process less uncertain.

Outreach in action

Three ways VJL builds connection

01

Prison visits

Published information says VJL works to coordinate regular visits that bring Veteran-focused education and resources behind the wall.

02

Community outreach

VJL participates in outreach events that connect the mission with Veterans, families, organizations, and the broader community.

03

Resource partnerships

Relationships with Veteran organizations help widen the network of information and support available during re-entry.

Visit frequency and available resources may change. Contact VJL for current outreach information.
