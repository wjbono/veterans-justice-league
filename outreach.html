VJL Program

Outreach

Setting the standard for what positive re-entry looks like and working to stop recidivism one Veteran at a time.

Prison Visits & Veteran Resources

Veterans Justice League currently states that it attempts to coordinate one to three prison visits a month to bring in Veteran resources and educate Veterans behind the wall about community resources available to them because of their Veteran status.

The purpose is to help create education and stability at an earlier point for Veterans who will be re-entering society.
