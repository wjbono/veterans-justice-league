Behind-the-Wall Training

Peer-driven training connecting active-duty service members with justice-involved Veterans who share lived experience around addiction, high-risk choices, incarceration, and consequences.

Fort Carson’s Behind The Wall Training

Who

Fort Carson Army Substance Abuse Program (ASAP), Veterans Justice League, and the Colorado Department of Corrections.

What

A 45-minute video teleconference and speaking engagement connecting Soldiers attending the two-day ADAPT Class with prior Soldiers who have made choices involving drugs or alcohol and have been incarcerated in the Colorado Department of Corrections.

Purpose

The published program description says the training is intended to facilitate discussion between active-duty service members and justice-involved Veterans serving prison sentences. Veterans discuss the events and behaviors that led to conviction and how those choices affected the things they value.

Peer Coaching & Prevention

VJL describes the program as a peer-led approach to helping active-duty service members understand the realities of alcohol and drug addiction and other high-risk behaviors and choices. VJL also describes the work as giving justice-involved Veterans purpose through sharing their experiences and reconnecting them with a military community they care about.

Turning Our Mess into The Message

VJL says this work has helped create a community of Veterans and active-duty service members who are often forgotten or fall through the cracks. The published program description says several Veteran participants have helped mentor active-duty service members and the VJL team has run multiple physical training sessions for active-duty service members.
