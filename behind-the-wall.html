Behind-the-Wall Training

Real experience. Honest consequences. Earlier intervention.

Justice-involved Veterans share the realities behind high-risk choices, addiction, incarceration, and the impact on what matters most.

Request Training InformationHow It Works
Prevention through lived experienceVeterans · Service members · Community

The purpose

Turn experience into prevention

The program creates direct conversation between active-duty service members and justice-involved Veterans. Participants hear how choices involving drugs, alcohol, and other high-risk behavior affected freedom, relationships, service, and the things Veterans value.

Why peer-led?

Lived experience makes the consequences concrete. It also gives justice-involved Veterans a meaningful way to reconnect with and support the military community.

Published training format

What the Fort Carson program includes

01

Partners

Fort Carson Army Substance Abuse Program, Veterans Justice League, and the Colorado Department of Corrections.

02

Connection

A video teleconference and speaking engagement connects Soldiers in the ADAPT Class with prior Soldiers who have been incarcerated.

03

Conversation

Veterans discuss behaviors that led to conviction, the consequences that followed, and what they wish others understood sooner.

Program format and availability may change. Contact VJL for current training and partnership information.

Peer coaching & prevention

Turning the mess into the message

VJL describes this work as helping active-duty service members understand addiction and high-risk behavior while giving justice-involved Veterans renewed purpose through service, mentoring, and connection.

Published program information also describes Veteran participants mentoring active-duty service members and VJL facilitating physical training sessions.
