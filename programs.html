Veterans Justice League

Programs

VJL focuses on housing, peer-led prevention training, and outreach supporting justice-involved Veterans and active-duty service members.

Program Areas

Explore each area of Veterans Justice League's published work.

VJL Housing

Veteran-specific housing supporting transition from the criminal justice system through structure, peer support, physical training, and community service.

Explore VJL Housing →

Behind-the-Wall Training

Peer-driven training connecting active-duty service members with justice-involved Veterans who share lived experience around high-risk choices and incarceration.

Explore Training →

Outreach

Prison visits, Veteran resources, community education, and outreach intended to create earlier stability and support positive re-entry.
