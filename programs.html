Each program has a distinct role, but all three are built around Veterans, lived experience, and long-term stability.

01

Stability & re-entry

VJL Housing

Veteran-specific housing supports the transition from the criminal justice system through structure, peer support, physical training, and community service.

  • Structured transition support
  • Veteran peer connection
  • Accountability and community
Explore VJL Housing
02

Experience-led prevention

Behind-the-Wall Training

Justice-involved Veterans share lived experience with active-duty service members, creating honest conversations about high-risk choices and incarceration.

  • Peer-led perspective
  • Real consequences, clearly shared
  • Prevention through connection
Explore Training
03

Resources & connection

Outreach

Prison visits, Veteran resources, and community education help create earlier stability and support a more positive re-entry.

  • Prison and community outreach
  • Veteran resource connection
  • Education and re-entry support
